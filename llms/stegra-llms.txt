# Stegra

> Stegra (formerly H2 Green Steel) is a Stockholm-headquartered industrial company building
> a large-scale near-zero-emission steel plant in Boden, northern Sweden. It replaces coking
> coal with green hydrogen from a giga-scale electrolyzer running on renewable Nordic
> electricity, integrating hydrogen production, direct-reduced green iron and steelmaking on
> one 270-hectare site. Its products are green steel, green iron and green hydrogen.

## Important

Stegra publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-29 there is no developer portal, no API
reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no MCP server and no A2A agent card on any
Stegra-controlled host. Do not attempt to call a Stegra API — there is none to call.
Commercial enquiries about green steel, green iron or green hydrogen go through the contact
form, not through an integration.

Not to be confused with **stegra.io**, an unrelated Swedish motorcycle-GPS app company.

## Engineering

- [stegraab on GitHub](https://github.com/stegraab): Stegra's public GitHub organization.
  Four repositories, all internal infrastructure tooling — Terraform providers and a tflint
  ruleset. These are clients for third-party vendor APIs, not SDKs for a Stegra API.
- [stegraab/stepca](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/stegraab/stepca): Terraform
  provider for Smallstep step-ca, authored by Stegra. v0.1.1, published 2026-04-01.
- [stegraab/nutanix](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/stegraab/nutanix): Stegra-patched
  fork of the Nutanix provider. v2.4.2-stegra.8, published 2026-07-09.
- [stegraab/sra](https://registry.terraform.io/providers/stegraab/sra): Stegra-maintained fork
  of BeyondTrust's Secure Remote Access provider. v1.3.0-stegra.1, published 2026-08-28.
